# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free learning and research environment. Be respectful,
assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, document evidence, and make room for learners
with different backgrounds.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate misinformation,
publication of private information, or sustained disruption. Report concerns privately to the
project maintainers. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
needed to protect the community.

Created by School of AI and School of QC.
